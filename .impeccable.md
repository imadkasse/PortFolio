## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience**: Recruiters and hiring managers reviewing fullstack developer candidates
- **Context**: Viewing on desktop and mobile during job screening
- **Goal**: Land interviews by showcasing skills, projects, and professional credibility

### Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Creative, bold, distinctive
- **Tone**: Memorable and confident, not generic
- **Emotional Goal**: Make a lasting impression that stands out from typical developer portfolios

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Style**: Bold & distinctive - strong visual presence, unique typography, dynamic layouts
- **Theme**: Dark only - sleek, developer-focused aesthetic
- **Key Differentiator**: Avoid "AI slop" - nothing generic or templated

### Design Principles
1. **Bold Typography**: Use distinctive display fonts, avoid safe choices like Inter/Roboto
2. **Strategic Color**: Use accent colors purposefully - dark backgrounds with high-impact accents
3. **Motion with Purpose**: Animations should enhance UX, not just decorate
4. **Avoid Generic Patterns**: No side-stripe borders, gradient text, glassmorphism, or card overload
5. **Show, Don't Tell**: Project cards should be visually compelling with clear CTAs
